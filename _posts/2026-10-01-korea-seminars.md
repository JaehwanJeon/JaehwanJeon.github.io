During my recent visit to Korea, I gave two BK seminars on trustworthy AI for structural safety, sharing my research and discussing potential applications with researchers in civil engineering and naval architecture and ocean engineering.

## Yonsei University — September 30, 2026

At the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering, I presented **“Trustworthy AI for Nonlinear Structural Dynamics: Physics, Uncertainty, and Automation.”** The talk covered physics-informed structural response prediction, uncertainty quantification, and the automation of structural reliability analysis.

Thank you to **Prof. Taeyong Kim** for the invitation and to everyone who joined the seminar and discussion.

## Seoul National University — October 1, 2026

At the Department of Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering, I presented **“Trustworthy AI for Structural Safety: Computation, Uncertainty, and Automation.”** We discussed how these approaches could extend to problems in naval architecture and ocean engineering, including offshore wind and offshore substation systems. These conversations offered useful perspectives on connecting my research with the challenges of offshore structures and energy infrastructure.

I am grateful to **Profs. Youngsub Lim, Do-Kyun Kim, and Youngmin Choo** for hosting me and for the opportunity to exchange ideas with faculty and graduate students.

![Group photograph with seminar participants at Seoul National University's Department of Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering on October 1, 2026.](/images/posts/2026-10-01-korea-seminars/snu-naoe-group.jpg)

During the visit, I also reconnected with my Ph.D. research group and gave a brief research presentation at a joint seminar with colleagues from Guangzhou University. Thank you to my Ph.D. advisor, **Prof. Junho Song**, for the invitation.
