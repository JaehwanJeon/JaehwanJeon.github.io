From efficient structural analysis to trustworthy and autonomous structural engineering

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Civil and Mineral Engineering at the University of Toronto. I received my Ph.D. in Civil and Environmental Engineering from Seoul National University in 2026, advised by Prof. Junho Song.

Structures are never crash-tested. A building is built once, and people move in the next day, so its safety exists only as a prediction. My research makes that prediction fast and trustworthy, in three directions:

  • Physics-informed AI: building mechanics into deep learning so that response predictions are fast and constrained by physics.
  • Uncertainty quantification: measuring how much each prediction can be trusted, and separating what more data or better models can reduce.
  • Analysis automation: LLM agents that choose a model, run validated solvers, and check the result.
